What it does

SiteCam is a worksite safety ops console: Live feed with PPE class chips and zone overlays, an Events CRM for PPE miss / intrusion / near-miss, Zones for work/exclusion/pedestrian/forklift paths, and notify-first automations.

PPE is hard hat / vest present or absent — category signals, never person identity. Near-miss (forklift vs pedestrian proximity) is notify + clip first; multi-frame before any escalate. No “Name this person” CTA — ever.

Industrial clarity tone: light win energy on empty / first setup; firm chrome on near-miss and breach. Dry-run keeps rehearsal from firing sirens until armed.

Honesty / trust

PPE classes ≠ who; near-miss notify-first.

  • PPE classes ≠ who — category, not identity; never claim who someone is.
  • Near-miss = notify-first; don’t soft-pedal breaches if armed.
  • No face ID day one; no “recognized Mike” language.
  • Dry-run ≠ live notify/siren storm until armed.
  • Sleeping ≠ failed; unbound hardware ≠ error.
  • Post worksite camera notice where required.
