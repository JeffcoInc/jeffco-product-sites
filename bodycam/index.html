What it does

BodyCam is a mocap-style presence console: multi-person body pose on Live, an anonymous People CRM, zone presence regions, and an Activity event stream for enter/exit/stillness/fall-hint.

People are ephemeral anonymous IDs (e.g. `P-17`) — never faces or names on day one. A persistent Anonymous mode banner keeps the product honest. Optional gameplay avatar export binds pose to sinks with dry-run.

Face linkage, if ever offered, follows QueueCam/Compliance BLOCK rules — disabled chrome, no Ready, never wizard-enabled. Anti-shell: refuse stick-figures-only Live without Zones + People + Activity.
