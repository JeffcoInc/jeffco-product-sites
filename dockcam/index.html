What it does

DockCam is a freight dock console: Live, Bays, Moves, Automations. Truck-at-bay and door open/closed are primary signals; pallet activity is motion/presence hints — not SKU truth.

LPR cross-link optional for plates on moves. Distinct from BoatDockCam marina metaphors.
